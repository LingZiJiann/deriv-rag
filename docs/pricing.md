## How Billing Works

- Billing is monthly and renews on the same calendar day each month.
- Usage is counted by the number of API requests made within the billing period.
- Overage on paid plans is charged at **$0.0001 per additional request**.
- Free plan requests do not roll over; they reset at the start of each billing cycle.

## Upgrading or Downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from **Settings > Billing**.

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately; you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Your current plan remains active until then.

## Cancellation

- Cancellations can be made at any time from **Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan**.
- Access continues until the end of the current paid period; no partial refunds are issued.
- Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do you offer annual billing?**
Yes. Annual plans are available at a 20% discount. Contact sales@example.com to switch.

**Q: Is there a free trial for paid plans?**
Yes. The Starter and Pro plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my monthly limit on the Free plan?**
Requests beyond the 10,000 monthly limit are blocked until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade.

**Q: Are taxes included in the listed prices?**
Prices shown are exclusive of VAT/GST. Applicable taxes are added at checkout based on your billing address.
